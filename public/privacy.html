Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026. This page explains, in plain language, what information YT Transcript ("this site", "we") uses when you visit, and what we do not collect.

1. What this tool does with your input

When you paste a YouTube link, the link's video ID is sent to our server, which asks YouTube for that video's public caption files and returns them to your browser. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or any account login to use the tool. There are no user accounts on this site.

2. Data we store

We do not keep a database of visitors. To make repeat requests fast, fetched transcripts are kept in a temporary server memory cache for a short time and then automatically discarded. Our server keeps only standard, short-lived technical logs needed to prevent abuse (for example, counting requests per IP address to enforce rate limits). We do not sell, rent or share personal information with anyone for marketing.

3. Cookies

The tool itself works without setting tracking cookies. However, third-party services used on this site — in particular Google AdSense advertising — may set cookies in your browser, as described in the next section. Your browser settings let you view, block or delete cookies at any time.

4. Google AdSense and third-party advertising

This site is supported by advertising served through Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of the DART cookie enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings: google.com/settings/ads.
  • You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info/choices.
  • Google's advertising requirements and how Google uses data from sites that use its services are described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

We do not control the cookies set by advertising partners. If advertising is not yet enabled on this site, this section describes how it will work once ads begin serving.

5. Third-party links and embedded services

Transcript timestamps link out to youtube.com, and thumbnails are loaded from YouTube's image servers. When your browser loads those, YouTube/Google receives your IP address as with any normal web request, under their own privacy policy. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites.

6. Children

This site is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your choices

You can use this tool without providing any personal information. If you contact us by email, we will use your email address only to reply to you. You may ask us to delete any email you have sent us at any time.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact

For any privacy question, use the Contact page.

YouTube Video Ki Transcript Kaise Nikale — Hindi Guide

Transcript matlab video mein jo kuch bola gaya hai, uska pura text — line by line, samay (timestamp) ke saath. Padhai, notes banane, ya apne video ke liye subtitles chahiye hon, to ye guide aapke kaam aayegi. Sab kuch phone se, bilkul free.

Step by Step — Bas 4 Step

  1. Video ka link copy karo. YouTube app mein video kholo, Share dabao, phir Copy link. Computer par browser ke upar wale address bar se link copy ho jata hai.
  2. Is site ke home page par link chipka do aur Get Transcript button dabao.
  3. Bhasha chuno. Agar video mein Hindi captions hain, to list mein Hindi dikhega — use chuno. "Auto" likha ho to matlab wo captions YouTube ne awaaz sunkar khud banaye hain.
  4. Copy ya Download karo. Copy se pura text copy ho jayega, Download .txt se file save hogi, aur Download .srt se subtitle file milegi jo video editing mein kaam aati hai.

Kaam ki Baatein

Timestamp par tap karo to video seedha usi line se chalega — lamba video dobara dekhne ki zaroorat nahi. Search box mein koi shabd likho, to transcript mein woh shabd jahan-jahan aaya hai, highlight ho jayega. Lecture aur class ke videos ke liye ye sabse kaam ka feature hai.

Agar Transcript Na Mile To?

Kuch videos mein captions hi nahi hote — na creator ne daale, na YouTube ne banaye. Aise video ki transcript koi tool nahi bana sakta, wahan "no captions" ka message dikhega. Kabhi-kabhar YouTube server ko rok deta hai, tab thodi der baad Try Again dabane se kaam ho jata hai. Private ya age-restricted (18+) video ki transcript bhi nahi mil sakti.

Hindi Captions Mein Galti Ho To?

Auto-generated Hindi captions mein kabhi-kabhi shabdon ki galti hoti hai, khaaskar naam aur English shabdon mein. Isliye transcript ko kahin publish ya submit karne se pehle ek baar padhkar theek kar lein. Creator ke khud daale hue (manual) captions sabse sahi hote hain — language list mein wahi pehle dikhte hain.

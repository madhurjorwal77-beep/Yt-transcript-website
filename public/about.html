About YT Transcript

YT Transcript is a small, free web tool built in India. It does one job well: turn a YouTube video's captions into a clean transcript you can read, search, copy and download.

Why this tool exists

Students revising from lecture videos, viewers who prefer reading to watching, creators writing descriptions for their own uploads, and people who are deaf or hard of hearing — all of them need video content as text. YouTube shows captions on screen, but copying them out is slow and downloads are not offered. This tool closes that gap in a few seconds.

How it works

When you paste a link, our server asks YouTube for the video's caption tracks, converts them into timestamped lines, and shows them in your browser. Nothing is uploaded by you, and there are no accounts. If a video has no captions, we tell you plainly instead of making something up.

Who made it

The site is designed, built and maintained by Madhur, an independent creator from India, as part of his tools-for-creators work around his YouTube channel. It is a personal project — not a company product — and it is not affiliated with YouTube or Google in any way.
